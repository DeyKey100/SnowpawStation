CRISP COZY WINTER - Free Sampler
================================

24 soft, cozy winter and holiday sound effects for games - footsteps on snow and ice,
snowballs and snowmen, skating, the fireside and cocoa, gifts, bells, decorations, a winter UI set,
winter magic, original jingles and seamless ambience loops. Version 1.0.

GET THE FULL PACK
  This free sampler has 24 of the 472 sounds.
  The full pack adds the other 448: https://anipixels.itch.io/crisp-cozy-winter
  Or get every Crisp pack (sounds, UI kits, icons, VFX) in one bundle:
  https://itch.io/s/203391/crisp-everything-bundle

FORMATS
  WAV/  44.1 kHz, 16-bit, stereo (for editing / highest quality)
  OGG/  Ogg Vorbis (small files, ready for Godot, Unity, GameMaker, web)

Every one-shot is loudness-matched, starts on its first sample (no dead air) and peaks at or below
-1 dBFS. Loops start and end on the same level and join without a click.

TIPS
  - Sounds ending in _01, _02, _03 ... are takes of one sound: pick one at random each time, so steps, snowballs
  -     and bells never sound robotic (the Godot sound bank does this for you).
  - Footsteps: 8 surfaces, 12 footstep sets - walk and run on powder snow, packed snow, icy crust and ice; walk on
  -     deep snow, slush, frosty grass and a snowy porch - 6 takes each, plus boot stomps and landing in snow.
  - Loops: everything in Ambience plus sled_loop and skate_loop loops seamlessly - set your player to loop.
  - ui_hover and ui_text_tick are mixed quiet on purpose.
  - Everything tuned is in C major pentatonic (C D E G A), the same as Crisp Cozy, so the two packs stay in tune
  -     together and jingles can play over any UI sound.
  - Tuned ladders climb in order: handbell_01..05, ui_slider_01..05, ui_tab_01..04, snowflake_pickup_01..05.
  - Every jingle and music-box tune is an original melody written for this pack - no carols, no existing songs.
